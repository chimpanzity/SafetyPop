Race model  Can strict side alternation alone produce the B completion rate?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Assume the ape alternates sides on every touch, starting on the side he actually touched first, with oddity touches drawn from his own forced 4-circle cost distribution for that polarity. Bubbles are cleared on the 8th bubble touch, so oddity wins the race if its array clears within 8 oddity touches (start on oddity) or 7 (start on bubbles).
   White: predicted completion by oddity 86%, observed 28% (n = 40 B trials; 10% started on oddity)
   Black: predicted completion by oddity 51%, observed 18% (n = 40 B trials; 20% started on oddity)
   Actual switch rate on core trials 7%: the closer to 100%, the less completion says about choice.
