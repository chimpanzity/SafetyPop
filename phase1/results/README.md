# Results — Phase 1

Outputs of the analyses in `analysis/`, built from `../data/processed/`. Nothing here is edited by
hand, and nothing here is an input to anything else.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `SafetyPop_Experiment1_Summary.pdf` | Three-page findings summary for Experiment 1: dataset, the within-trial abandonment result, the discrimination null, every preregistered hypothesis with its outcome |
| `figures/SafetyPop_Figure1_opting_out.png`, `.pdf` | Four-panel summary figure. A: what a single opt-out button would have missed. B: survival by escape cost. C: hazard by session block. D: per-subject error sensitivity |
| `figures/SafetyPop_abandonment_hazard.png` | Working figure from `analysis/hazard_analysis.py`: pooled hazard, hazard by escape cost, per-subject odds ratios, hazard by session block |

## Status of these numbers

Descriptive and, except where they map onto the preregistration, exploratory. The event-history
and cost models were developed after data collection and are labelled as exploratory in the
summary. With five subjects, between-subject correlations are indicative only.

The scripts that produced the summary PDF and Figure 1 are not yet in `../analysis/` (see that
folder's README). The figures and PDF here are the versions reviewed on 2026-11-24.
